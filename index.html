✦ Glimmerly

Privacy Policy

Nothing leaves the device.

Effective September 10, 2026 · last updated September 17, 2026

Glimmerly: Early Learning is a learning app for young children. It was built with one rule from day one: everything runs on the device, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. This page explains exactly what that means.

✓What Glimmerly does and doesn't do

It does

Store your child's progress (which topics are completed, an optional first name) in the app's local storage, on the device only.

It does not

Connect to the internet, use a backend, create accounts, show ads, use analytics or tracking SDKs, or share data with anyone — including us.

💾What's stored, and where

Glimmerly saves a small amount of information using your device's local storage — the same mechanism a website uses to remember a setting, except here it never leaves the app:

DataPurposeLeaves the device?
Which topics you've completedShow a checkmark on finished topics and each subject's progressNever
The subject of your most recently finished lessonContinue where you left offNever
Child's first name (optional)Personalize the on-screen greetingNever
Sound, music & quiz-timer preferencesRemember volume, mute, and timer settingsNever

That's the complete list. There is no account, no email address, no device identifier, no location, no camera or microphone access, and no advertising ID involved at any point.

👥Children's privacy

Glimmerly was built for a first grader and is designed to be safe for children to use on their own. Because no data ever leaves the device, there is nothing to collect, store on a server, or share — which means there is nothing that requires parental consent under COPPA or similar children's privacy laws. A grown-up can view or clear everything the app has stored at any time from the device's own app settings.

🔒Permissions

The Android app does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive device permission.

✎Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that changes what's stored — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly. Given the app's offline-by-design approach, no change is expected to introduce any data collection or sharing.
